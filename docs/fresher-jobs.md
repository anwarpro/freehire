# Fresher jobs

The jobs page highlights a Fresher shortcut above the list. It selects the existing
`intern` and `junior` seniority values and requires at most one year of stated
minimum experience, including roles whose experience requirement is unspecified.
The copy asks readers to check the original posting; the badge is a search
classification rather than a promise that every graduate is eligible.

The shortcut preserves country, source, skills, text, and other filters. Turning
it off clears the seniority and experience selections. The ordinary URL and saved
search serialization also applies to this preset, including pagination and alerts.
Changing individual filters can turn the preset indicator off.

Full job rows show a Fresher badge under the same seniority/experience rule.
Compact tracking cards do not carry experience requirements and receive no badge.
No schema, search settings, worker, or reindex change is required.
